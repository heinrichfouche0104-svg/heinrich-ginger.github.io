Privacy policy

For Clip Engine and this website. Last updated 24 October 2026.

The short version

Clip Engine is private software that one person — Heinrich Fouche — runs on his own computer to publish his own videos. It has no other users, no accounts to sign up for, and no servers. This website is three plain pages and collects nothing about you.

What this website collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no forms, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies set by this site. The pages are static files hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep its own standard server logs, which are covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

What Clip Engine accesses, and why

Clip Engine works with the owner's own accounts, with his permission, to do the job he asks of it:

  • YouTube. With Google sign-in, it uploads the owner's own videos to his own channels, and reads the view and watch-time figures for those videos so he can see how they did.
  • Transcription and clip selection. Audio from the owner's own recordings is sent to ElevenLabs for transcription, and transcripts are sent to Anthropic's Claude to pick the moments worth clipping. Both are used under their own terms as service providers.

It does not read anyone else's channel, account or content, and it does not touch accounts belonging to other people.

Where the data lives

Footage, clips, transcripts, settings and sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's own computer and external drive. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except the videos the owner chooses to publish to his own channels. There is no central database, and no one else has access.

YouTube API Services

Clip Engine uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner is agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information obtained from the YouTube API is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show him their performance. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and it is not combined with data from anywhere else. Figures pulled from YouTube are kept on the owner's computer only for as long as they are useful, and are deleted when the related clip is deleted.

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google security settings page, which revokes Clip Engine's permission immediately.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. The only parties who ever see any of it are the service providers named above, each doing the specific job asked of them.

Children

Clip Engine is private software for one adult, and this website is not aimed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
