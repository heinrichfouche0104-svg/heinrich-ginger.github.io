How it gets made

I built the production system myself. It takes the footage off the cameras, lines up every angle against the microphone audio, transcribes it, picks the moments worth keeping, cuts and captions them, and publishes on a schedule. One shoot becomes a week of content without an editor.

That's also why the reviews are worth reading: when I write about a tool, it's usually because it's load-bearing in something I actually shipped.
